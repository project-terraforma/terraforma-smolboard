Terraforma

Methodology

Geoconflation benchmark

Models are evaluated on whether two geospatial records refer to the same place. Results are summarized with F1 score, accuracy, precision, recall, latency, and token usage.

Metric handling

Higher-is-better metrics are plotted directly. Latency is normalized inversely for radar visualization while the original seconds value remains available in tooltips. Axes without measurements for any model are omitted; missing measurements for individual models are left blank rather than shown as zero.
