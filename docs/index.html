Terraforma SMOLBoard

The world's first global leaderboard benchmarking LLMs on geoconflation

What is geoconflation?

Geoconflation is the process of combining multiple sources of geospatial data into one unified, accurate dataset for geographic information systems.

Our Mission

Terraforma SMOLBoard creates a unique benchmarking system measuring LLM performance based on how accurately they can determine whether two different sets of geodata are referring to the same place.
